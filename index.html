THE BITS stands for Brilliant IT Solutions, a startup founded by a team of Information Technology students who share one goal: building technology that is both functional and meaningful. Just as a bit is the smallest unit of digital information, we believe even the smallest ideas can grow into impactful solutions. Our latest project, MyFairview, is an IoT-based disaster risk reduction and emergency response system built for Barangay Fairview, Quezon City.
